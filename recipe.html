Pan Seared Steak

Jump to Recipe ↓

Garlic and rosemary butter turns this pan-seared steak into something worth lingering over. It has a deep, caramelised crust and a tender, juicy centre, and it's easier than you'd think.

The Best Pan-seared Steak

Ready in 20 minutes, all in one pan, with no oven needed. This is the steak we come back to all year round: quick, simple, and finished with a garlic butter you'll want to spoon over everything.

Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 steaks, about 3 cm thick
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary

Steps

  1. Take the steaks out of the fridge and let them come to room temperature for about 30 minutes.
  2. Season both sides of the steaks with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a cast-iron skillet over high heat until very hot. Add olive oil.
  4. Place the steaks in the skillet and sear for 3-4 minutes on each side for medium-rare, or until desired doneness.
  5. Add butter, garlic, and rosemary to the skillet. Spoon the melted butter over the steaks for extra flavor.
  6. Remove the steaks from the skillet and let them rest for 5 minutes before slicing and serving.
